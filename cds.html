ABOUT CDS

CDS (Combined Defence Services) is a national-level examination conducted by UPSC (Union Public Service Commission). It is for graduates who want to become officers in the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force.

CDS Academies

  1. IMA – Indian Military Academy (Army)
  2. INA – Indian Naval Academy (Navy)
  3. AFA – Air Force Academy (Air Force)
  4. OTA – Officers Training Academy (Army)

LOCATION OF ACADEMY

Academy Jagah Force
IMA (Indian Military Academy) Dehradun Army
INA (Indian Naval Academy) Ezhimala Navy
AFA (Air Force Academy) Hyderabad Air Force
OTA (Officers Training Academy) Chennai Army (Short Service)

Eligibility

  • IMA aur OTA ke liye kisi bhi stream mein graduation chahiye.
  • INA ke liye Engineering degree chahiye.
  • AFA ke liye graduation mein ya 12th mein Physics aur Maths hona zaroori hai.
  • Age har academy ke liye alag hoti hai, lagbhag 19 se 25 saal ke beech.
  • Final year ke students bhi apply kar sakte hain.

Latest details hamesha UPSC ki website par check karo.

Exam Pattern

Paper Time Marks
English 2 hour 100
General Knowledge 2 hour 100
Elementary Maths 2 hour 100
SSB Interview 5 day 300

Maths ka paper IMA, INA aur AFA ke liye hota hai. OTA mein sirf English aur GK hota hai.

Galat jawab par negative marking hoti hai.

CDS Study Plan

Time Kya karna hai
6:00 - 7:00 AM Running aur exercise
8:00 - 9:30 AM English (Grammar, Vocabulary, Comprehension)
10:00 - 11:30 AM Elementary Maths
4:00 - 5:30 PM General Knowledge (History, Polity, Geography, Science)
7:00 - 8:00 PM Current Affairs
9:00 - 10:00 PM Revision aur mock test

Tips:

  • English aur GK mein achhe marks laana sabse zaroori hai, kyunki ye har academy ke liye hain.
  • Roz newspaper padho.
  • Har hafte ek mock test do aur galtiyan note karo.
