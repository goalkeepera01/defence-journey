Physical Fitness

Defence mein selection ke liye padhai ke saath fit hona bhi zaroori hai.

Daily Exercise Weekly Plan Diet Tips

Fitness kyu zaroori hai?

Written exam clear karne ke baad SSB interview aur medical test hota hai. SSB mein ground par kai physical activities hoti hain, aur academy mein training bhi bahut mehnat wali hoti hai. Isliye abhi se fitness par kaam shuru karna chahiye.

  • Stamina badhta hai, jisse lambi training aasan lagti hai.
  • Body strong rehti hai aur chot lagne ke chance kam hote hain.
  • Padhte waqt dimaag bhi fresh rehta hai.

Daily Exercise

Exercise Shuruaat ka Target Fayda
Running 1 km se shuru karo, dheere dheere badhao Stamina aur dil ki sehat
Push-ups 10 ke 3 set Chest aur haath
Sit-ups 15 ke 3 set Pet ki muscles
Pull-ups 2 se 5, jitne ho sake Back aur haath
Squats 15 ke 3 set Pair ki taakat
Skipping 5 minute Body ki speed aur balance

Shuruaat mein jitna ho sake utna hi karo, phir har hafte thoda badhao.

Weekly Plan

Din Kya karna hai
Monday Running + Push-ups
Tuesday Sit-ups + Squats + Skipping
Wednesday Running + Pull-ups
Thursday Push-ups + Sit-ups
Friday Running + Squats
Saturday Khel khelo (football, cricket, volleyball)
Sunday Aaram, halki walking ya stretching

RUNNING TIPS

Ek achha target 2.4 km ko kam se kam time mein poora karna hota hai. Pehle din se itna mat daudo, step by step badhao:

  • Week 1: 1 km, dheere dheere running.
  • Week 2: 1.5 km.
  • Week 3: 2 km.
  • Week 4: 2.4 km, aur ab time note karna shuru karo.

Uske baad koshish karo ki har hafte time thoda kam ho.

Diet Tips

WHAT TO EAT

  • Milk, curd and paneer
  • Eggs or sprouts
  • banana, apple and orange
  • Dal, roti and rice
  • Green vegetables

WHAT NOT TO EAT

  • Junk food
  • Excessive fried food
  • Soft drinks
  • Too many sweets
  • Packaged snacks

WATER AND SLEEP

  • Drink enough water throughout the day.
  • Stay hydrated during exercise.
  • Get 8 hours of sleep daily.

IMPORTANT POINT

  • Exercise se pehle 5 se 10 minute warm-up karo, aur baad mein stretching karo.
  • Chot lage ya dard ho to ruk jao, aur zarurat ho to doctor ko dikhao.
  • Roz thoda karna, kabhi kabhi bahut zyada karne se behtar hai.
  • Selection ke time medical test hota hai, isliye apni sehat ka dhyan rakho aur aankhon ki bhi jaanch karwate raho.
