What is NDA?

The National Defence Academy (NDA) is one of India's premier joint training institutions for future officers of the Indian Army, Navy and Air Force. Through the NDA examination conducted by UPSC, eligible candidates can begin their journey towards becoming commissioned officers in the Indian Armed Forces after completing their 12th standard..

Eligibility

  • 12th pass ya 12th mein padh rahe ho.
  • Air Force aur Navy ke liye 12th mein Physics aur Maths hona zaroori hai.
  • Age lagbhag 16.5 se 19.5 saal ke beech honi chahiye.
  • Unmarried hona zaroori hai.
  • Ladke aur ladkiyan dono apply kar sakte hain.
